TrustChain Keyboard: Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · Трастчейн Туул ХХК (Trustchain Tuul LLC), Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia

Summary

TrustChain Keyboard collects no data. Everything you type stays on your device.

What the keyboard does and does not do

  • The iPhone keyboard runs without "Full Access" (it never asks for it), so iOS does not allow it to use the network.
  • The Android keyboard has no internet permission.
  • It does not record, store, send, or share anything you type: no keystroke logging, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts.
  • The only thing it saves on your device is ordinary keyboard state, such as whether caps lock is on, and never the text you type.
  • The companion app shows setup steps and a test field. Text in that field stays on your device and is not saved.

Website

The page at trustchain.mn/keyboard (the web keyboard) runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing you type anywhere. Our server keeps standard web-server access logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security and operations.

Children

The keyboard collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
